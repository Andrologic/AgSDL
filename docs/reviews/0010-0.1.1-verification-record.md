## Limits

The specification, schemas, operation editions and `agsdl-0.1.0` marker remained
unchanged. Only processor editions advanced to 0.1.1. Five E/G/I differences
remained open: aggregates after missing AgentBinding identity, extra fields
without Step.kind, Instructions.target, Instructions.at and the aggregate after
missing Skill.tools. U+E000/U+10000 collation was also unresolved. Existing
Unicode cases do not prove general ordering agreement. The
[diagnostic dossier](0009-0.1.1-diagnostic-expectations.md) retains the details.

These finite checks do not establish execution, readiness, interoperability or
general conformance. This record attests to local verification, not publication.
