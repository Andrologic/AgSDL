# Decision 0013: adopt the bounded AgSDL 0.2.0 contract

- Status: accepted under delegated maintainer authority; applied locally, unreleased.
- Reviewed application base: `bc441c0a894d286c4585eee261b07f8f03f9d6a7`.

## Authorization and selection

The maintainer renewed the instruction to "boucler jusqu'à ce que ça soit prêt".
Under that delegation, the orchestrator selects the reviewed bounded 0.2
contract for local adoption, following the delegation precedent in
[Decision 0007](0007-adopt-0.1.0-contract.md). This records a delegated selection;
it does not claim that the maintainer individually approved every detail of the
candidate or every earlier proposal. Publication is excluded and requires
separate authorization.

The selected requirements are the index and three chapters in `spec/0.2/` at
the reviewed application base. Their proposal basis is
[0019](../../proposals/0019-agent-prompt-and-resources.md),
[0020](../../proposals/0020-blueprint-flow-0.2.md) and
[0021](../../proposals/0021-logic-block-contract-0.2.md), with accepted directions
in [Decision 0011](0011-message-based-agent-model.md) and
[Decision 0012](0012-blueprint-flow-directions.md). The adopted boundary is the
concrete reviewed contract, not every broader alternative or historical proposal.
The review examined lifecycle, content, modular declarations, migration,
independent declaration readers and their report comparison before this selection.

## Normative application

The [0.2.0 specification](../../spec/0.2/README.md) is the normative source.
Its document and report marker is `agsdl-0.2.0`. Promotion changes the edition
marker and maintained paths, not the reviewed semantic algorithms or rules.
The active derived schema, examples, readers and corpus move to `tooling/0.2/`.
Report comparison conventions remain non-normative; this adoption creates no
additional operation-feature catalog or whole-system conformance profile.

The contract covers persistent Agents, Messages and content roles, retained
configuration, optional Interfaces, explicit URI origins and scoped support
claims. Optional flow covers Agent work, Call, Condition, Prepare, bounded Joins,
queueing and steering, response attribution, closed failures, non-nested local
composition and protected admission. Its declared integration obligations do
not imply that the repository implements execution or enforces authority.

## Historical and evidence boundaries

The pre-adoption candidate `agsdl-exp-flow-0.2-c1` remains available at the
application base. Its artifacts and retained reports keep their original
identity. A 0.2.0 reader rejects that marker; prepare a separate document and
run fresh checks rather than changing source bytes or relabelling past reports.
[Migration](../0.2/migration.md) records the mechanical candidate transition and
the more substantial mapping from 0.1 and the frozen 0017 experiment.

The normative 0.1 text, its schemas/readers/corpus, frozen experiments and earlier
decisions retain their bytes and meaning. AgSDL 0.1.1 remains the latest published
release. The [0.2.0 release notes](../releases/0.2.0.md) remain UNRELEASED and
separate bounded verification evidence from execution claims.

No runtime, universal interoperability, content retrieval, provider integration,
remote operation, tag or publication follows from this decision. Actual delivery,
continuity, effects, stopping and authority require consuming implementation
evidence for the claimed behavior.
