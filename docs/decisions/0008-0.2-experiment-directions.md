# Decision 0008: authorize bounded 0.2 prototype directions

- Status: accepted authorization for an experiment; no normative adoption.
- Date: 2026-09-22.
- Integrated starting base: `6f503b687f30f23f2a9a6fef0a17f5a6e8cc2677`.

## Mandate

After the brainstorming and its HTML presentation, the maintainer authorized:
"Vas y je te laisse procéder avec tes recommandations".
This records authorization to test the six directions below, not approval of
every later serialization or diagnostic detail. The concrete experimental rules
are proposed in [0016](../../proposals/0016-kiss-experiment-0.2.md). That proposal
is the single candidate rule source; this decision records the mandate and limits.

1. One common edition for optional standard parts; presence triggers their
   checks. External engine, adapter and extension identities remain open.
2. Test whole embedded values or references only for Instructions and Interface,
   with identical content/ports/application points and explicit operation and
   Application addressing. No override, merge or anonymous Agent/graph model.
3. Exact local Definition identity is frozen artifact plus local id. Compute
   the artifact hash outside its bytes. Language edition is separate; even
   formatting or configuration-only edits change exact artifact identity.
4. Principal may be absent in a descriptive Agent. Governed uses require an
   explicit actor by a checkable trigger. Absence means undeclared, with no
   inferred permission or engine identity. Existing actors are not erased.
5. Keep Agent, content, graph, configuration, Tool contract and implementation
   distinct. One explicit binding per Agent/configuration, no default engine or
   inheritance. Keep incompatible choices visible; claims are not runtime proof.
6. Test compact scoped reports with exact input identity, stable diagnostics and
   prevented checks with causes. Validation does not require inventory, complete
   success traces or exchange. Implement only the bounded cases defined in 0016,
   not every proposed behavior in 0015.

## Limits and evidence condition

The local work first produces a precise candidate and exact examples, then
independent readers and comparison evidence in separately assigned work. This
first lot does not implement readers. A favorable documentary review shows only
that the experiment is suitable to implement, not that it works interoperably.
The proof stage needs two independent implementations, adversarial as well as
positive inputs, and agreement on candidate observations. Disagreement or an
underspecified case requires correction and review of the candidate before a
claim of agreement; no reader's behavior silently becomes the rule.

Skills, imports, packages, assemblies, parallelism, dynamic delegation, macros,
runtime, hot reload, automatic migration, editor and product integrations are
excluded from this experiment. These deferrals do not remove future 0.2 scope.
Execution still follows stop, modify and start a new execution; no approval
survives a changed configuration pin. No automatic migration can erase actors,
effects or content. No source is rewritten or labelled losslessly converted by
this authorization.

[0014](../../proposals/0014-minimal-core-0.2.md) and
[0015](../../proposals/0015-validation-reports-0.2.md) retain their proposed status
and bytes. [Decision 0007](0007-adopt-0.1.0-contract.md), the published contract,
official schemas, readers, corpora and historical snapshots remain unchanged.
The published release is still 0.1.1 with contract `agsdl-0.1.0`, as recorded in
[release status](../../README.md#release-status-and-history).

Normative adoption, publication, deployment and execution support require separate
decisions and evidence. This mandate authorizes none of them.
